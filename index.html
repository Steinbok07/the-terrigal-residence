02 — The Residence

A New Expression of Coastal Living

Elevated above Terrigal’s celebrated coastline, this comprehensively transformed residence brings together sweeping coastal outlooks, contemporary architectural detailing and exceptional everyday comfort.

Across three thoughtfully connected levels, natural finishes, carefully composed interiors and expansive glazing establish an effortless relationship between the home and its coastal setting.

03 — The Outlook

A View Worth Coming Home To

Expansive glazing draws the coastline into everyday life, creating an ever-changing backdrop of ocean, sky and coastal landscape.

From the first light of morning to the softer tones of evening, the outlook becomes an integral part of the home’s architecture — elevated views across Terrigal and towards Wamberal, and generous balconies that carry the living spaces outdoors.

04 — Living & Entertaining

Spaces Designed to Live Beautifully

Defined by clean lines, warm materials and beautifully resolved details, the living spaces offer a contemporary interpretation of coastal comfort.

Carefully considered lighting and cabinetry add character without competing with the extraordinary outlook.

  • Bespoke timber cabinetry
  • Stone benchtops
  • Integrated architectural lighting
  • Timber flooring
  • Contemporary fixtures

05 — Private Retreats

A Private Retreat Above the Coast

Framed by a soaring raked ceiling and expansive glazing, the master bedroom opens onto its own elevated balcony, drawing in natural light and beautiful coastal views.

A peaceful retreat where architecture and outlook come together.

Quiet Luxury in Every Detail

Private spaces continue the residence’s considered design language, pairing warm natural textures with refined contemporary finishes.

Beautifully detailed bathrooms and thoughtfully finished interiors establish an atmosphere of calm and comfort.

Contemporary craftsmanship.

06 — Connected Living

Three Levels. Seamlessly Connected.

Designed for both everyday convenience and the years ahead, a private internal lift connects the garage with the main living and bedroom levels.

Architectural staircases, natural timber detailing and carefully integrated lighting complement this seamless connection between spaces — making it easy to move between levels with shopping, luggage or simply at a gentler pace, now and for the long term.

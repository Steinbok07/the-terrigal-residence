02 — The Residence

A New Expression of Coastal Living

Elevated above Terrigal’s celebrated coastline, this comprehensively transformed residence brings together sweeping coastal outlooks, contemporary architectural detailing and exceptional everyday comfort.

Across three thoughtfully connected levels, natural finishes, carefully composed interiors and expansive glazing establish an effortless relationship between the home and its coastal setting.

The Terrigal Residence is a four-bedroom, three-bathroom home for sale in Terrigal, on the New South Wales Central Coast. Extensively renovated, it offers elevated ocean views, a private internal lift serving three levels and a double garage.

The Residence in Brief

Where is the home?
In Terrigal, a coastal village on the Central Coast of New South Wales. The residence sits in an elevated position with views across Terrigal and towards Wamberal.
How many bedrooms and bathrooms?
Four bedrooms and three bathrooms. The master bedroom has a robe, an ensuite and its own balcony. The top level holds a study or fourth bedroom with its own ensuite and balcony.
Does it have ocean views?
Yes. The living room, the master bedroom and the balconies look over the rooftops to the ocean and the curve of the coastline.
Is there a lift?
Yes. A private internal lift connects the garage with the main living level and the bedroom level, three stops in all.
What about parking?
A double garage at street level, with a lobby to the lift and a cellar on the same level.
How is the home laid out?
The garage level opens to the lift. Above it, the living level holds the kitchen, dining and lounge, opening to a balcony. The bedroom level has three bedrooms, the family room, a bathroom, the laundry and a balcony. The top level holds the study or fourth bedroom.
What has been renovated?
The home has been extensively renovated with contemporary architectural detailing: a kitchen with stone benchtops and bespoke timber cabinetry, bathrooms with a fluted bath and twin-basin vanity, timber flooring, feature stair lighting and integrated architectural lighting throughout.
How can I inspect it?
Inspections are held privately. See Private Inspections below to arrange one.

02 — The Residence

A Four-Bedroom Residence. Your Ideas Welcome.

With a deposit taken on Unit 2, Unit 1 is the last opportunity to secure your place at The Terrigal Residence — and the timing opens a conversation about how you want to live.

The interior transformation is planned to begin in mid-December 2026, with the residence expected to be ready for market in March 2027. Talk directly with the developers while the design can still be shaped.

Start with a four-bedroom design inspired by completed Unit 2, or discuss a different arrangement — including the possibility of making the second floor a full-sized parents’ retreat. An internal lift like Unit 2’s is optional, subject to the agreed design and specification.

The Opportunity in Brief

Which residence is available?
Unit 1 at 55 Kurrawyba Avenue, Terrigal. A deposit has been taken on Unit 2, leaving one opportunity at The Terrigal Residence.
Can I see the finished result?
Yes. Visit completed Unit 2 by appointment as the display home. The photographs on this site show Unit 2 and provide a reference for design discussions.
Could Unit 1 be a carbon copy?
Discuss reproducing Unit 2’s design and finish, or adapting the interiors to suit your lifestyle. The scope and specification will be agreed with the developers.
What design ideas can we discuss?
A four-bedroom residence is the starting point. Another possibility is a full-sized parents’ retreat on the second floor. Layout changes depend on design feasibility and the agreed scope.
Is a lift included?
A lift like the one in Unit 2 is optional. Discuss its configuration and cost with the developers.
When does the transformation happen?
Interior works are planned from mid-December 2026, with a target of being ready for market in March 2027. The programme is subject to the agreed works.
How do I arrange a visit?
Use the enquiry form to request a display-home visit and a conversation with the developers about Unit 1.

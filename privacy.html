Privacy Statement

Last updated 11 October 2026

This statement explains how personal information is handled when you enquire about The Terrigal Residence through this website. The website is operated by the owner of the residence.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we collect the details you enter: your name, email address and, if you choose to give them, your phone number and message. If you call or email directly, we receive your phone number or email address in the usual way.

Why we collect it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, arrange a private inspection and send you information about the residence that you've asked for. We don't sell your details, add you to a mailing list, or share them with anyone for marketing.

How enquiries are sent

The enquiry form is delivered by Web3Forms, a form-processing service, which passes your message to the owner's email inbox. Web3Forms and our email provider may store and process your details on servers outside Australia. See the Web3Forms privacy policy.

Advertising and measurement

This website may use Google Ads conversion tracking to measure whether people who arrive from our advertisements go on to enquire, call or email. This uses cookies and similar technology. It records that an enquiry or contact click occurred, but doesn't send Google the contents of your enquiry. You can manage Google's use of advertising cookies at adssettings.google.com, or block cookies in your browser.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept only as long as needed to deal with the sale of the residence, then deleted.

Access, correction and questions

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or ask any question about this statement, using the contact details on the home page.

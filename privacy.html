Privacy Statement

Last updated 11 October 2026

This statement explains how personal information is handled when you enquire about The Terrigal Residence through this website. The website is operated by the owner of the residence, Gavin Yates.

What we collect

When you send an inspection enquiry, we collect the details you enter: your full name and email address and, if you choose to give them, your mobile number, a preferred inspection date and a message. If you call or email Gavin directly, he receives your phone number or email address in the usual way.

Why we collect it

Your details are used only to respond to your property enquiry: to reply, arrange a private inspection and send information about the residence you've asked for. We don't sell your details, add you to a mailing list, or share them with anyone for marketing.

How enquiries are delivered

The enquiry form is processed by Formspree, a form-handling service. Formspree checks the submission for spam and emails it to Gavin. Formspree and our email provider may store and process your details on servers outside Australia, including in the United States. See the Formspree privacy policy.

Advertising and measurement

We advertise this residence with Google Ads. To see whether the advertising works, this website may use Google Ads conversion tracking and Google Analytics. These use cookies and similar technology to record visits, and whether a visitor sends an enquiry or taps the phone number or email address. They don't send Google the contents of your enquiry. If you arrive from one of our ads, your enquiry email notes that, with Google's reference for the ad click, so we can tell which advertising works. Google's use of this information is described at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can manage Google ad personalisation at My Ad Center.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept only as long as needed to deal with the sale of the residence, then deleted.

Access, correction and questions

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or ask any question about this statement. Email gavin@steinbok.com.au or call 0417 687 697.
